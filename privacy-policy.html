Privacy and data protection

Privacy policy
for Storax.

Storax is a file manager. This page explains what it keeps on your device, what it sends to Google Firebase, and which connections it makes when you use a feature.

  • Last updated: November 17, 2026
  • Applies to Storax for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Back to home

At a glance

  • Your files stay on your device. Storax has no file upload service of its own.
  • Only files you put in a vault are encrypted. Other files are not encrypted by default.
  • The Android, iPhone and Mac apps send anonymous reports about app use and crashes to Google.
  • There is no setting in the app to turn those reports off yet.
  • Smart features download the files they need only when you turn them on.
  • We do not sell your information.

01

Who we are

Storax is a product of Kumpali Private Limited. This policy covers the Storax app and this website. It does not cover third-party apps, websites or services you connect to from Storax, such as a file server or a cloud storage app.

02

What stays on your device

Storax works with the files on your device. Unless a feature says otherwise, these files stay on your device and are not uploaded to us. The app stores the following locally:

  • Your files and folders. Storax reads, moves, copies and deletes them when you ask it to.
  • Vault and hidden items. Items you hide are locked with encryption that needs your PIN and this device. See section 06.
  • Trash and history. Deleted items wait in Trash until you restore or remove them. Operation history is kept so you can undo some actions.
  • Saved server logins. If you save a connection to a file server, its address, username and password are stored on your device.
  • Trusted servers. Storax remembers the servers you have trusted, so it can warn you if one of them changes.
  • Search data. Storax keeps lists of file names, text and small previews so search and browsing are faster. These stay on your device.
  • Smart-feature downloads. Files that smart features download are stored on your device.
03

What we and Google receive

The Android, iPhone and Mac versions of Storax use Google Firebase, a Google service, to send anonymous reports about how the app is used, when it crashes, and how fast it runs. The Windows and Linux versions do not send these reports.

Usage events

Storax notes taps and actions so we can see which features people use. Each report includes which screen you were on, your language and country settings, the time, and your time zone.

A tap can include the words on the item you tapped, up to 60 characters, so it may include a file name.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, Google receives details about what went wrong and where in the app it happened, along with your phone model, its system version, and the screen that was open.

Speed measurements

Google also measures how long screens take to open and how long internet requests take.

What is not included

These reports do not include your files, what is inside them, where they are stored, your passwords, your PIN or anything in your vault. They do not include your location. Google gives each installed copy of the app a random number to tell reports apart. Storax has no accounts and does not attach your name or email to these reports.

Your choice

There is currently no setting in the app to turn these reports off. The only way to stop them is to uninstall the app.

05

Permissions

Storax asks for permissions only for the features that use them. Examples:

  • All-files access lets the file manager see and change files across storage.
  • Location is required by Android to find nearby devices over Wi-Fi. It is used only on your device and is never included in reports.
  • Notifications show progress for transfers and background work.
  • Fingerprint is used only if you turn on fingerprint unlock.
  • Accessibility setting (App Lock only). If you turn on App Lock, Storax uses Android's Accessibility setting to notice which app you open. It uses this only to ask for your PIN or fingerprint before a locked app opens. Storax does not read what is on your screen, record what you type or act on your behalf, and this information never leaves your device.
06

How we protect your data

Files in the vault are locked with strong, modern encryption that needs both your PIN and a secret key kept on this device. After too many wrong PINs, the vault locks for a while.

That wait resets if the app is restarted. The device key is normally kept in your phone's secure storage area. If that is not available, Storax keeps it in an ordinary app file instead, which is less protected and may be included in your phone's automatic backups.

Storax cannot recover a vault whose PIN you have forgotten, or hidden items on a device that is lost or reset. Keep your PIN somewhere safe.

07

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers named in section 04 (Google Firebase, and the model host Hugging Face) so they can run their part of the app, and when the law requires it. Google may handle this data in countries other than your own.

08

Your choices

  • Uninstalling Storax removes the app's data from your device. Files you saved to your device storage remain.
  • You can turn off App Lock, and its Accessibility setting, in Android settings.
  • You can ask us about data that reached us through Google's reports. Because Storax has no accounts, we will ask you for enough detail to find the right records.
  • You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you.
