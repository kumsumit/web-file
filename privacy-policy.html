Privacy and data protection

Privacy policy
for Storax.

Storax is a file manager. This page explains what it keeps on your device, what it sends to Google Firebase, and which connections it makes when you use a feature.

  • Last updated: November 17, 2026
  • Applies to Storax for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Back to home

At a glance

  • Your files stay on your device. Storax has no file upload service of its own.
  • Only files you put in a vault are encrypted. Other files are not encrypted by default.
  • Release builds on Android, iOS and macOS send anonymous usage and crash reports to Google Firebase.
  • There is no in-app switch to turn that reporting off yet.
  • AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face only when you turn on a feature that needs them.
  • We do not sell your information.
01

Who we are

Storax is a product of Kumpali Private Limited. This policy covers the Storax app and this website. It does not cover third-party apps, websites or services you connect to from Storax, such as an SFTP server, an FTP server or a cloud app.

02

What stays on your device

Storax works with the files on your device. Unless a feature says otherwise, these files stay on your device and are not uploaded to us. The app stores the following locally:

  • Your files and folders. Storax reads, moves, copies and deletes them when you ask it to.
  • Vault and hidden items. Items you hide are encrypted with keys derived from your PIN and a device secret. See section 06.
  • Trash and history. Deleted items wait in Trash until you restore or remove them. Operation history is kept so you can undo some actions.
  • Remote connection profiles. If you save SSH, SFTP or FTP connections, their host names, usernames and passwords are stored on the device. The FTP password is stored with the profile.
  • SSH host keys. The fingerprints of servers you have trusted are stored so Storax can warn you if they change.
  • Search and media indexes. Storax keeps indexes of file names, text and thumbnails to make search and browsing faster. These are stored on the device.
  • AI models and downloads. Models you download are stored on the device.
03

What we and Google receive

Release builds on Android, iOS and macOS use Google Firebase to receive anonymous usage data, crash reports and performance measurements. Firebase is operated by Google. Windows and Linux builds do not send this data.

Usage events

Storax records taps and actions so we can see which features are used. Each event includes the screen name, your language and country setting, the event time, and your time zone offset.

A tap event can include the text of the item you tapped, up to 60 characters. A file row can therefore send a file name.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or records an error, Firebase Crashlytics receives technical details such as the error message, a stack trace, the device model and operating system version, and the screen that was open.

Performance measurements

Firebase Performance Monitoring measures timing data, such as how long screens and network requests take.

What is not included

The analytics code does not send your files, file contents, file paths, passwords, PINs or vault contents. It does not send your location. Firebase assigns the app an installation identifier. Storax does not set a user ID or create an account.

Your choice

There is currently no option in the app to turn this reporting off. Reporting is turned off in debug builds. To stop it on a release build, uninstall the app.

05

Permissions

Storax asks for permissions only for the features that use them. Examples:

  • All-files access lets the file manager see and change files across storage.
  • Location is required by Android for Wi-Fi and Wi-Fi Direct discovery. It is used on the device to find nearby devices and is not sent in analytics.
  • Notifications show progress for transfers and background work.
  • Biometric is used for unlocking when you turn on fingerprint unlock.
  • Accessibility service (App Lock only). If you turn on App Lock, Storax uses Android's accessibility service to see which app and screen are in the foreground. It is used only to show your PIN or fingerprint prompt before a locked app opens. Storax does not read on-screen content, record keystrokes or act on your behalf, and it does not send accessibility information off the device.
06

How we protect your data

Vault and hidden items are encrypted with AES-GCM. Keys are derived from your PIN with Argon2id and combined with a device secret. Wrong PIN attempts trigger a temporary lockout.

The lockout is kept only while the app is running, so restarting the app clears it. The device secret is normally stored in the Android Keystore. If the Keystore is unavailable, Storax stores the secret in a file in app storage instead, which is less protected. Auto Backup is not yet turned off for this file.

Storax cannot recover a vault whose PIN you have forgotten, or hidden items on a device that is lost or reset. Keep your PIN somewhere safe.

07

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers named in section 04 (Google Firebase, and the model host Hugging Face) so they can run their part of the app, and when the law requires it. Google Firebase may process data in countries other than your own.

08

Your choices

  • Uninstalling Storax removes the app's data from your device. Files you saved to your device storage remain.
  • You can turn off App Lock, and turn off the accessibility service, in Android settings.
  • You can ask us about data that reached us through Firebase. Because Storax has no accounts, we will ask you for enough information to find the relevant records.
  • You can ask us to delete any data we hold about you.
