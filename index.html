Core features

Everything you reach for, every day.

Hide what is private, find what you lost, clear out what you no longer need, and pass the rest to someone nearby, all without switching apps.

  1. 01

    Private vaults

    Move files and folders into a locked vault and they vanish from view until you open it with your PIN. Add a decoy vault for extra cover. Too many wrong PINs and the vault locks itself for a while.

  2. 02

    Mistakes you can take back

    Deleted files go to Trash first, so you get a second chance. Restore what you need, and undo or redo supported actions with a tap.

  3. 03

    All your media, sorted

    Photos, videos, music, GIFs, documents, zipped files and downloads each get a home of their own, with video previews and a list of recent files.

  4. 04

    Find anything, even by description

    Search by name, type, size or date. Find words inside your documents and even in photos, or simply describe what you are looking for and let smart search find it, right on your phone.

  5. 05

    Duplicates and lookalikes

    Spot duplicate files, duplicate folders and visually similar photos, and see exactly what is eating your space before you clear it.

  6. 06

    Share with anyone nearby

    Send files and text to a nearby phone or computer by scanning a code or sharing a link, or let Storax find it for you. On Android, two phones can even connect straight to each other, no Wi-Fi network needed. Every transfer is kept in your history.

Security and privacy

Private means locked, not just hidden.

Files in the vault are scrambled on your device, so they can only be opened with your PIN on this device. After too many wrong tries, the vault locks for a while.

  • Strong, modern encryption for everything in the vault
  • Locks for a while after too many wrong PINs
  • Decoy vault for an extra layer of privacy
  • Optional App Lock for the apps you choose

Keep your PIN safe

Vault items are tied to your PIN and this device. Forget the PIN or lose the device and they cannot be recovered, so keep your PIN somewhere safe.

What leaves your device

Storax does not upload your files anywhere. Smart features download what they need only when you turn them on. The Android and Mac apps send anonymous reports about app use and crashes to Google. See exactly what is sent.

Only what you allow

Storax asks to use your storage, notifications, location or nearby devices only when a feature needs it, such as finding a phone to share with.

Honest about limits

Some features depend on your device. Finding text in photos, App Lock and phone-to-phone sharing are only on Android. Trash and undo work a little differently on each system, and the computer versions focus on managing files.
